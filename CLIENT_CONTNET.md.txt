# Gordonridge Community Development Initiative

## HOME

### Building a Stronger Gordonridge Together

Gordonridge Community Development Initiative is a Black, resident-led organization working to strengthen families and improve the quality of life for residents in the Gordonridge community in Scarborough.

Our work brings generations together, with a strong focus on seniors and youth. Through intergenerational programs, community safety initiatives, family support, leadership development and connections to resources, we help residents build a stronger and more connected community.

# WHO WE ARE

Gordonridge Community Development Initiative was created by residents who understand the strengths, challenges and everyday realities of the Gordonridge community.

Gordonridge is a Toronto Community Housing community in Scarborough and is home to many Black families, including first- and second-generation Black immigrant families.

We believe residents should have a strong voice in shaping their own community. Our organization brings seniors, youth, families, volunteers and community partners together to address local needs, develop resident leadership and create opportunities for people to support one another.

---

# OUR MISSION

Our mission is to improve the well-being of Black families and residents in Gordonridge by connecting generations, strengthening families, developing local leadership and creating access to opportunities, resources and community support.

## Our Vision

A strong, safe and connected Gordonridge where seniors, youth and families are supported, residents have a voice, and different generations work together to strengthen their community.

## Our Values

Community • Inclusion • Leadership • Opportunity • Safety • Respect • Accountability

# WHAT WE DO

## Intergenerational Seniors & Youth Programs

Our seniors and youth programs bring different generations together to learn from one another, build relationships and reduce isolation.

Activities can include mentoring, storytelling, technology support, community meals, workshops, cultural activities, leadership opportunities and community projects.

Young people gain knowledge, guidance and positive relationships with older residents, while seniors remain active, connected and involved in the community.

## Seniors Engagement & Wellness

We create opportunities for seniors to remain active and connected through social gatherings, wellness activities, workshops, community meals, digital literacy, outings and peer support.

A major focus is reaching seniors who may be isolated or have difficulty accessing services and community activities.

## Youth Leadership & Development

We help young people build confidence, leadership skills and stronger connections to their community.

Youth can participate in mentoring, leadership development, volunteering, employment readiness, life-skills activities and community projects.

## Community Safety & Violence Prevention

We work with residents, youth, families and community partners to promote safer neighbourhoods.

Our approach focuses on prevention, positive relationships, resident involvement, conflict prevention and connecting young people and families with appropriate supports.

## Family & Community Support

We help residents connect with food support, employment opportunities, housing and community resources, workshops, referrals and other services.

Our goal is to make it easier for families to find help when they need it.

## Resident Leadership & Volunteerism

Residents are not only participants in our programs; they are part of leading the work.

We create opportunities for seniors, youth, parents and other residents to volunteer, develop leadership skills, participate in community decision-making and help shape Gordonridge initiatives.

# WHERE WE SERVE

## Gordonridge Community

Our work is centred in the Gordonridge Toronto Community Housing community in Scarborough, including residents and families around 10, 20, 30 and 40 Gordonridge Place.

Our community activities are centred around the Community Centre at 20 Gordonridge Place.

We also work with schools, community organizations, service providers, government, businesses and other partners serving Gordonridge and surrounding Scarborough communities.

# COMMUNITY IMPACT

## Generations Working Together

Our goal is to build a community where residents feel connected, supported and involved.

Through our work, we aim to:

- Reduce social isolation among Black seniors.
- Build stronger relationships between seniors and youth.
- Develop youth and resident leaders.
- Increase volunteering and community participation.
- Connect families with resources and services.
- Create positive opportunities for young people.
- Improve community safety and belonging.
- Help seniors become more comfortable using technology.
- Connect residents with employment and other opportunities.
- Strengthen the voice of Gordonridge residents.
