What Our Work Aims to Achieve

Rather than relying on abstract numbers, Gordonridge Community Development Initiative measures its real-world success through the tangible improvements in connection, dignity, leadership, and safety experienced by residents.

diversity_1
Pillar 01

CONNECTED GENERATIONS

  • check_circle Reduce social isolation among Black seniors and elders who live alone.
  • check_circle Build stronger relationships between seniors and youth through shared storytelling, mentoring, and community meals.
  • check_circle Help seniors become more comfortable using technology to access essential services and video call family.
military_tech
Pillar 02

OPPORTUNITY & LEADERSHIP

  • check_circle Develop youth and resident leaders who take active roles in guiding community programs.
  • check_circle Create positive opportunities for young people through athletic recreation, mentoring, and life-skills workshops.
  • check_circle Connect residents with employment, training, internships, and educational opportunities.
shield
Pillar 03

STRONGER COMMUNITY

  • check_circle Increase volunteering and community participation across all four residential towers.
  • check_circle Connect families with food support, housing, and social services to reduce daily stress.
  • check_circle Improve community safety and belonging, and strengthen the collective voice of Gordonridge residents.
Resident-Led Change

Building Trust One Neighbor at a Time

When seniors and youth sit together at the Community Centre on Gordonridge Place, barriers dissolve. Youth discover trusted elder mentors who believe in their potential; seniors regain active social vitality and a renewed sense of purpose.
