Partner With Us

Strong communities are built through partnership. Gordonridge Community Development Initiative welcomes collaboration with organizations committed to strengthening Black families and Scarborough residents.

How Partners Support Gordonridge

Gordonridge Community Development Initiative welcomes partnerships with government, foundations, corporations, schools, community organizations, service providers and local businesses.

Partners can support our community through funding, volunteers, employment opportunities, workshops, technology, professional expertise, donations and collaborative community initiatives.
